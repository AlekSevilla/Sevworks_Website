Good Beta

An AI-powered tool for rock climbers. Good Beta helps you work out your next route so you spend less time guessing and more time climbing.

The idea

What is "beta"?

In climbing, "beta" is the information that helps you figure out a route: where to put your hands, where to step, and how to sequence the moves. Good Beta uses AI to help climbers get that guidance.
