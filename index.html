About

Built in Texas.

Sevworks is an independent software studio based in Texas. We build our own products and partner with people who need software done right.

Whether it's an AI tool for climbers or a safe place for kids to play, we hold every app to the same standard: useful, honest, and easy to use, with no dark patterns and no selling your data.

  • Honest software. No dark patterns and no unnecessary data collection.
  • Craft over volume. We take on fewer projects and finish them properly.
  • Built to last. Clean, maintainable code you can grow with.
